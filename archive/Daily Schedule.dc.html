Daily classroom schedule

How the day moves

Snacks are provided by the school, morning and afternoon. Lunch comes from home. The order stays predictable so children know what happens next.

Rainy day? Still outside.

A large covered lanai holds the sandbox, easels and special programs, so the day keeps its rhythm whatever the weather does.

Good to know

Before the first day

Pack a lunch

Lunch comes from home. Both snacks are provided by the school.

Dress for sand

Play clothes and closed shoes. Children are outside first thing every morning.

Rest things

A blanket and a small comfort item for nap time, labeled with your child's name.

Toilet trained

Required for enrollment, for children two to six years of age.
